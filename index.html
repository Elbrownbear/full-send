Controls

Phone

Drag left/right: steer
Tap: ollie
Flick up: hard stop + spray
In the air: swipe to spin, hold to grab
Second finger: instant ollie

Keyboard

Left/Right or A/D: steer
Down/S: point downhill
Up/W: hard stop + spray
Space: ollie (hold in air to grab)
Left/Right in air: spin
P: pause · R: restart

Mouse

Point where to go
Above the rider: hard stop
Click: ollie

Spray

Spray skiers: clout + patrol heat
Spray snowboarders: lose clout
