Stance

Controls

Phone

Drag left/right: steer
Tap: ollie
Flick up: hard stop + spray
Pull thumb down: lean back (pow, tail press)
Lean + flick sideways: butter 180
In the air: swipe to spin, hold to grab
Second finger: instant ollie
Catwalk or tree well: tap fast

Keyboard

Left/Right or A/D: steer
Down/S: point downhill
Up/W: hard stop + spray
Space: ollie (hold in air to grab)
Shift: lean back (pow, tail press)
Shift + Left/Right: butter 180
Left/Right in air: spin
Catwalk or tree well: mash Space
P: pause · R: restart

Mouse

Point where to go
Above the rider: hard stop
Click: ollie · Right button: lean back

Snowboarding

Ollie onto rails and boxes to grind
Powder bogs you down unless you lean back
Trees in powder have tree wells
Catwalks are flat: keep your speed or skate
Ice: no turning, no stopping

Heat and Ski Patrol

Spray or plow skiers (50+ mph): clout + heat
Do it to snowboarders: lose clout
Slow Zones: over 25 mph = clout + heat, under = cool off
Duck a rope: fresh pow, clout, lots of heat
Max heat calls patrol. Outrun them to shake them
Past 2,000 m they come anyway. Caught = pass clipped
